# Showcase audio provenance

- `bed.wav`: original synthesized sound bed, generated by `scripts/prepare-audio.py` using sine waves. No third-party music or samples.
- `libro-slower.wav`: the single Spanish word **libro**, extracted from the locally generated Dictionary Audio neural pack and converted with `ffmpeg -af atempo=0.85`, preserving pitch. Synthetic Mexican Spanish, Piper `es_MX-claude-high`, 22,050 Hz, 16-bit. This is not a human recording or narration.
- Voice model card: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/es/es_MX/claude/high/MODEL_CARD (voice dataset listed as Apache-2.0 in the pack provenance).
- Piper engine: https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl (GPL-3.0).
- No full voice model, dictionary, private book text, English recording archive, or pronunciation pack is included.
